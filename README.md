# 📊 E-Commerce-Sales-Dashboard

The Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report designed to analyze and monitor sales performance across different products, customers, and regions. It provides real-time insights into key business metrics such as Total Sales, Profit, Orders, and Revenue Growth. The dashboard enables users to identify sales trends, track top-performing products, evaluate regional performance, and make data-driven business decisions through intuitive visualizations and dynamic filtering capabilities.

## Overview

An Ecommerce Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze ecommerce business performance through data-driven insights. The dashboard helps stakeholders monitor sales, profit, customer behavior, product performance, and regional trends in a single view.

## Project Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profitability.
* Track customer purchasing patterns.
* Monitor state-wise sales performance.
* Identify top-performing product categories and sub-categories.
* Evaluate monthly profit trends for business decision-making.

## Dashboard Features

### Sales Analysis

* Total revenue and profit tracking.
* Monthly profit trend analysis.
* Interactive filtering by Quarter and State.

### Customer Insights

* Top customers by sales amount.
* Customer purchasing behavior analysis.

### Product Performance

* Category-wise quantity distribution.
* Sub-category profit comparison.
* Identification of best-selling products.

### Regional Analysis

* State-wise sales performance.
* Comparison of revenue contribution across regions.

### Payment Analysis

* Distribution of orders by payment mode.
* Analysis of customer payment preferences.

## Tools & Technologies Used

## Key Business Insights

* COD (Cash on Delivery) is the most preferred payment method.
* Clothing contributes the highest sales quantity among categories.
* Maharashtra generates the highest sales revenue.
* Printers deliver the highest profit among sub-categories.
* Monthly profit trends reveal seasonal variations in business performance.
